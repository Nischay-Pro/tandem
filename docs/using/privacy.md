# Privacy policy

For anyone using the Tandem for Actual app on Android, Wear OS, iOS or Apple
Watch. Effective 28 November 2026.

Tandem is a client for your own [Actual Budget](https://actualbudget.org)
server. The developer runs no server for the app, keeps no account for you,
and receives none of your data.

## What Tandem collects

Nothing. Tandem has no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash reporting
service, and doesn't send your data to the developer or to anyone you didn't
choose.

## Where your data goes

Your budget lives on the Actual server you sign in to, and on your devices.
Tandem only contacts:

| Service                      | What it receives                                                                                      | When                                     |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Your Actual server           | Your budget, through Actual's own sync. Bank syncing also goes through it, to the providers you link. | Always                                   |
| Your extension sync server   | Encrypted planning details such as goals and debt terms, if you set it up.                            | Only if you set it up                    |
| Frankfurter (exchange rates) | Currency codes and dates, never amounts, to the public server or one you choose.                      | Only if you turn on Fetch exchange rates |
| Bank websites                | A request for the bank's logo. The bank learns that a device asked, nothing about your budget.        | Only if you turn on Show bank logos      |
| Your WebDAV server           | Copies of automatic backups, encrypted if you set up Encrypt backups.                                 | Only if you set it up                    |
| Pages in your browser        | Signing in with OpenID, linking a bank and links you tap open in your browser.                        | Only when you tap                        |

The app lists the same services under **About → Services Tandem connects to**.

## On your device

- Your budget copy, pending edits, settings and planning details are stored on
  your device. Encrypted budgets stay encrypted at rest and can require your
  password or biometrics to open.
- Receipt photos are read on your device and aren't uploaded anywhere.
- Watch apps get only the summaries and quick-add choices your phone sends
  them.
- Automation apps such as Tasker, Shortcuts or assistants can add
  transactions or read figures only after you turn that on in Settings.
- If the app crashes, a report may be kept on the device. It's only sent
  anywhere if you choose to share it.

## Permissions

Tandem uses the network to reach your servers, notifications for alerts you
turn on, and the camera only when you take a receipt photo. It doesn't ask for
location, contacts, SMS, call logs or files outside those you pick.

## Deleting your data

Remove the app, or use **Settings → Your data** to erase what's on the device.
Your budget stays on your Actual server, which you control.

## Children

Tandem isn't directed at children under 13.
